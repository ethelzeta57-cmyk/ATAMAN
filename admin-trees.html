What is an Existing Tree in ATAMAN?

In the ATAMAN Catbalogan City Reforestation Initiative ("Tanom. Ataman. Padayon."), an Existing Tree refers to an established standing native hardwood, semi-mature tree, or mature canopy tree already rooted within Catbalogan's protected watershed sectors. Unlike newly propagated nursery seedlings, existing trees already provide active slope stabilization, microclimate cooling, and wildlife habitat.

📏
Minimum Criteria

Height ≥ 1.5m, trunk diameter (DBH) ≥ 2.5cm, and established root system (≥ 1 year age).

📍
Sector Tagging

Assigned to official Samar Watershed Sectors & Plots (Zones 1–6) with verified GPS pins.

🤝
Adoption & Care

Eligible for community adoption and claimed by field caretakers in the live Caretaker Queue.

Priority Native Hardwood Species for Existing Trees

Narra (Pterocarpus indicus) • High timber & canopy value
Molave (Vitex parviflora) • Resilient coastal limestone hardwood
Yakal (Shorea astylosa) • Critically endangered Samar dipterocarp
Banaba (Lagerstroemia speciosa) • Watershed buffer & floral canopy
Tindalo (Afzelia rhomboidea) • Premium native lowland tree
Dao (Dracontomelon dao) • Massive buttressed riverbank protector

Existing Tree Life Stages & Classifications

🌱 Young Native Tree (1–3 yrs): Height 1.5m–3.5m, established beyond nursery stage, requires ongoing weed clearing and fertilization.
🌳 Semi-Mature Tree (3–10 yrs): Height 3.5m–8.0m, robust trunk, actively expanding canopy, excellent for community adoption sponsorship.
🌲 Mature Canopy Hardwood (10+ yrs): Height > 8m, primary watershed stabilizer, measured for annual carbon stock and seed source preservation.
👑 Centennial Heritage Tree: Iconic landmark trees of exceptional age or cultural significance in Samar protected under municipal ordinance.
