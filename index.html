Ecological Mission

Protecting Samar's Forests, One Native Tree at a Time

ATAMAN bridges the gap between civic sponsors, municipal reforestation authorities, and field caretakers. Every sponsored tree is planted in critical Samar watershed sectors with dedicated field monitoring.

🤝

1. Citizen Sponsorship

Individuals, families, and organizations can sponsor native trees starting at ₱50 per tree. Adopters receive official Certificates of Recognition and permanent digital GPS tagging in the Samar registry.

🧑‍🌾

2. Dedicated Caretakers

Field caretakers (such as Dodong Zeta in Canlapwas and June Luna in Darahuway Daco) are assigned to nurture each batch, logging regular weed control, fertilizing, and photographic proof of tree survival.

🗺️

3. Verified Land GIS Registry

No ghost trees or approximate markers. Every tree record has exact on-land coordinates across Catbalogan City watershed sectors and Darahuway Daco Island, monitored via live interactive maps.

Geographic Registry

Catbalogan City & Samar Sectors

Trees are strategically distributed across verified on-land sectors to form a continuous green protective belt around Catbalogan City.

Sector A

Brgy. Canlapwas — Central Watershed Reserve

Critical freshwater catchment zone anchored by native Narra and watershed saplings.

Sector B

Brgy. Guinsorongan — Coastal Green Buffer

Dense Molave hardwood zone protecting shoreline settlements against storm surges and coastal winds.

Sector C

Brgy. San Isidro — Ridge & Highland Reserve

Steep elevation corridor fortified by indigenous Yakal dipterocarps for erosion control.

Sector D

Brgy. Bunuanan — Biodiversity Forest Corridor

Enduring Tindalo trees connecting natural migratory pathways for Samar wildlife.

Sector E

Brgy. Mabini — Upland Conservation Sector

Deep forested hills hosting buttress-rooted Dao trees to safeguard local water tables.

Island Sanctuary

Darahuway Daco — Marine & Mangrove Sanctuary

Island reserve station featuring coastal buffer trees and flowering Banaba saplings.

Simple 4-Step Process

How Tree Adoption Works

From species selection to field care and growth tracking, your sponsorship directly contributes to Samar's canopy.

1

Choose Species & Quantity

Pick from 6 indigenous Samar species (Narra, Molave, Yakal, etc.) and select how many trees you want to sponsor.

2

Select Sector & Caretaker

Assign your trees to verified watershed locations (Canlapwas, Guinsorongan, etc.) maintained by dedicated field personnel.

3

Instant Official Certificate

Receive your personalized Samar Reforestation Certificate of Recognition with your Adopter ID and GPS tags.

4

Monitor Live in Dashboard

Access your dashboard anytime to view GPS coordinates, tree health indicators, and field photographic updates.
